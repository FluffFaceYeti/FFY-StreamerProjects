# FFY Socials

Timed pop ups for your socials. Make a list of your YouTube, Discord, Instagram and anything else, and they take turns popping up on stream. Got a promo going on? Add your own with a custom name and picture.

There's no Streamer.bot, no login and nothing to install. It's just an OBS browser source.

- **17 socials ready to go**, with their icons and colours
- **Custom pop ups** with your own name, text, colour and picture (animated GIFs work too)
- **Appear and disappear animations**, picked separately
- **Your own colours**, with one for everything or a different one for each pop up
- **Box or no box**, so you can have the icon and text floating on their own

## Setting it up

1. Download this whole **FFY_Socials** folder and keep everything in it together. The easiest way is **Code > Download ZIP** on the main repo page.
2. Open **`FFY_Socials-Settings.html`** in your browser.
3. Build your list and pick your look. The preview updates as you go.
4. Click **Copy link for OBS**.
5. In OBS, add a **Browser** source, paste the link into **URL** (leave **Local file** unticked) and set the size to **1920 x 1080**.

If you change anything later, or move the folder, copy the link again and paste it over the old one.

## Using your own pictures

Put them in the **`images`** folder, then click **Choose** next to the pop up you want them on. You can also paste a full `https://` link to a picture instead.
