# FFY Socials

A little widget that pops up your socials on a timer. Make a list of your YouTube, Discord, Instagram or whatever else you've got, and they take turns appearing on stream. If you've got a promo running, you can add that too with its own name and picture.

There are 17 socials ready to go, and you can pick the colours and how each pop up appears and disappears. Don't want a box behind it? Switch it off and the icon and text float on their own.

## Setting it up

1. Download this whole folder and keep everything in it together. The easiest way is **Code > Download ZIP** on the main repo page.
2. Open **`FFY_Socials-Settings.html`** in your browser, build your list and pick your look. The preview updates as you go.
3. Click **Copy link for OBS**.
4. In OBS, add a **Browser** source, paste the link into **URL** and set the size to **1920 x 1080**. Leave **Local file** unticked.

If you change something later or move the folder, copy the link again and paste it over the old one.

## Using your own pictures

Drop them in the **`images`** folder, then click **Choose** next to the pop up you want them on. Animated GIFs work too. You can also paste a full `https://` link to a picture instead.
