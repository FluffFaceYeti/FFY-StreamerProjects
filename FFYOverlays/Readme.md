# FFY Overlays

Free OBS overlays for your stream. Each one is a single HTML file: no Streamer.bot, no login, nothing to install.

## FFY Beard Raid

**[`FFY-BeardRaid.html`](FFY-BeardRaid.html)** turns every raid into a beard battle. You and the raider appear on screen with tiny beards. Your chat spams your emotes, the raiders spam theirs, and every emote flies in and grows that side's beard. When the timer runs out, the biggest beard wins the crown.

Your emotes and the raider's are detected automatically, including 7TV and BTTV emotes.

### Setup

1. Download `FFY-BeardRaid.html` and open it in your browser.
2. Type your Twitch channel name, pick a battle length and your beard colours, then click **Preview** to see it in action.
3. Click **Get OBS link** to copy your link.
4. In OBS, add a **Browser** source sized **1920 × 1080**, leave **Local file** unticked and paste the link into the URL box.
5. Put it at the top of your scene. It stays invisible until a raid comes in.

### Commands

You and your mods can use these in chat:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!beardtest` | Runs a test battle with fake emotes |
| `!beardbattle @someone` | Starts a battle against that channel, no raid needed |
| `!beardstop` | Ends the current battle early |
