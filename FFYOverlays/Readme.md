# FFY Overlays

OBS overlays for your stream. Each one is just a single HTML file, so there's no Streamer.bot, no login and nothing to install.

## FFY Beard Raid

**[`FFY-BeardRaid.html`](FFY-BeardRaid.html)** turns raids into a beard battle. You and the raider pop up on screen with tiny little beards. Your chat spams your emotes, the raiders spam theirs, and every emote flies over and grows that side's beard. Biggest beard when the timer ends gets the crown.

It works out whose emotes are whose by itself, and that includes 7TV and BTTV ones.

### Setting it up

Download `FFY-BeardRaid.html` and open it in your browser. Type in your Twitch channel, choose how long the battle lasts and pick your beard colours. Hit **Preview** if you want to see it in action first.

When you're happy, click **Get OBS link** to copy the link. In OBS, add a **Browser** source at **1920 × 1080**, leave **Local file** unticked and paste the link into the URL box. Put it at the top of your scene. You won't see anything until someone raids.

### Commands

You and your mods can use these:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!beardtest` | Runs a test battle with pretend emotes |
| `!beardbattle @someone` | Starts a battle against that channel, without a raid |
| `!beardstop` | Ends a battle early |
