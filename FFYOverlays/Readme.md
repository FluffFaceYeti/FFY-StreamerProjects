# FFY Overlays

These are the overlays I've made for OBS. You don't need Streamer.bot or an account for any of them. You open the page in your browser, set it up how you like, copy the link it gives you and paste that into a Browser source in OBS.

Each one has its own folder with its own instructions:

- **[FFY Raid Battles](FFY-RaidBattles)** turns raids into a battle between your chat and the raiders.
- **[FFY Socials](FFY-Socials)** pops up your socials and promos on a timer.
