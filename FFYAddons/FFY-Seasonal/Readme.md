# FFY Seasonal

Commands for different times of the year. Each one is its own import, so you can add Halloween in October, Christmas in December, and so on.

You'll need **FFY Commands** installed first, as these all plug into it.

| Season | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **[FFY Valentines](FFY-Valentines/Readme.md)** | `!lovestruck`, cheesy `!pickupline`s, `!date`, `!serenade @someone`, a `!cupidarrow @someone` duel and Valentine of the Day. 14 commands |
| **[FFY Easter](FFY-Easter/Readme.md)** | `!egghunt`, `!easteregg`, `!bunnyname`, `!eggtoss @someone`, an `!eggrace @someone` egg and spoon race and Easter Bunny of the Day. 11 commands |
| **[FFY StPatricks](FFY-StPatricks/Readme.md)** | `!irish`, `!lucky`, `!potofgold`, `!jig`, `!pinch @someone`, a `!jigoff @someone` dance-off and Leprechaun of the Day. 10 commands |
| **[FFY Summer](FFY-Summer/Readme.md)** | `!sunburn`, `!icecream`, `!getaway`, `!beachfind`, `!splash @someone`, a `!sandcastle @someone` contest and Lobster of the Day. 12 commands |
| **[FFY Halloween](FFY-Halloween/Readme.md)** | `!costume`, `!pumpkin`, `!spooky`, `!candy`, `!trickortreat`, `!spook @someone`, and some Nightmare Before Christmas ones like `!jack` and `!oogieboogie`. 17 commands |
| **[FFY Bonfire Night](FFY-Bonfire-Night/Readme.md)** | `!fireworks`, `!sparklerword`, `!toffeeapple`, `!rocket @someone`, a `!fireworkoff @someone` and Guy of the Day. 11 commands |
| **[FFY ThanksGiving](FFY-ThanksGiving/Readme.md)** | `!turkey`, `!gobble`, `!pie`, `!thankful`, a `!wishbone @someone` duel and Turkey of the Day. 12 commands |
| **[FFY Christmas](FFY-Christmas/Readme.md)** | Santa's naughty or nice list, `!present`, `!grinch`, `!reindeer`, `!snowball @someone`, Grinch of the Day and a few more. 16 commands |
| **[FFY New Year](FFY-New-Year/Readme.md)** | `!resolution`, `!prediction`, `!partyhat`, `!cheers @someone`, a `!countdownclash @someone` and Party Animal of the Day. 11 commands |

## Installing

Check **[FFY Commands](../../FFYCommands/Readme.md)** is installed, then open the season's folder and download its file. In Streamer.bot, click **Import** at the top, drag the file in and click **Import**.

When the season's over, open its folder in the **Commands** tab and untick **Enabled** on the commands (or just delete them). You can import it again next year.
