# FFY Web Giveaway

Your own ticket giveaway website for Twitch, hosted for free. Viewers can enter even while you're offline.

- **Viewers** log in with Twitch, see their points and buy tickets.
- **Points** are earned automatically. Everyone in your chat gets points every few minutes while you're live. Or use your Streamer.bot FFY_Points balance instead.
- **Your control page** lets you start a giveaway, set a draw date, see entries, and draw, redraw or cancel with refunds. It works from your phone too.
- **Chat messages** go out when a giveaway starts and when the winner is drawn, plus every entry if you want.
- **The OBS overlay** pops up the winner with confetti and their win chance.
- **Your look:** banner, profile picture, background, points name and icon, and colours.

Everything is yours. It runs on your own free GitHub Pages and your own free Supabase project, so nobody else holds your viewers' points.

This one isn't a Streamer.bot import. It's a website you set up once, in about 15 minutes.

---

## Getting it

### 👉 [Make my own copy](https://github.com/new?template_name=FFYGiveaway&template_owner=FluffFaceYeti)

Name it `giveaway`, set it to **Public**, and click **Create repository**. Then follow the setup guide.

- **[Setup guide](https://github.com/FluffFaceYeti/FFYGiveaway/blob/main/SETUP.md)**: seven steps, start to finish.
- **[Setup helper](https://flufffaceyeti.github.io/FFYGiveaway/setup.html?new)**: fill in your details and it writes the fiddly bits for you, with copy buttons.

You'll need free accounts on **GitHub** and **Supabase**, plus your Twitch.

---

## Using your own domain

Got a website already? You can put the giveaway on a subdomain like `giveaway.yoursite.com`. The setup guide covers it at the end.
