# FFY Add-ons

Extra bits for your stream. Every add-on is a separate import, so just grab the ones you like the look of.

| Add-on | What you get | Needs FFY Commands? |
|---|---|---|
| **[FFY Seasonal](FFY-Seasonal/Readme.md)** | A pack for each time of year, each one its own import: Valentine's, Easter, St Patrick's, Summer, Halloween, Bonfire Night, ThanksGiving, Christmas (Santa's naughty or nice list is in there) and New Year | Yes |
| **[FFY Game Packs](FFY-GamePacks/Readme.md)** | Commands for whatever game you're on, one import per game: Fortnite, Minecraft and Gears Of War | Yes |
| **[FFY Daily's](FFY-Dailys/Readme.md)** | 20 daily channel point redeems, like *"Bob has redeemed their daily head pat!"*. Viewers can claim each one once a day. Twitch only | No |
| **[FFY Pokemon](FFY-Pokemon/Readme.md)** | Open Pokémon booster packs (10 cards plus an Energy) with PSA grades and prices. There are god packs, ten 1-1 Pikachus, `!keep` / `!sell all` / `!sell bulk`, `!binder`, `!bestcards`, `!pokemonwallet` and `!pokemontop`, and `!pokemonseason` resets everything after however many days you pick | No |
| **[FFY Dino](FFY-Dino/Readme.md)** | `!dig` up fossils and finish dinosaur skeletons for your `!museum`. Amber, 1-1 legendary fossils, `!bones`, `!sellbones`, `!bestfossils` and a season reset | No |
| **[FFY Mining](FFY-Mining/Readme.md)** | `!mine` for ores and gems, `!sellores` for coins and `!upgrade` your pickaxe to find better stuff. BIG FIND diamonds, 1-1 gems, `!bestgems` and a season reset | No |
| **[FFY Pets](FFY-Pets/Readme.md)** | 27 kinds of pet, from dogs, cats and chickens up to Highland Cows, Mooshrooms and a Baby Dragon. Either chat shares one pet, or everyone adopts their own with `!registerpet`. Then it's `!feedpet`, `!walkpet`, `!playpet`, `!trainpet` to teach tricks, `!pettrick`, `!cuddlepet`, bond levels and easy on/off switches | No |
| **[FFY Tamagotchi](FFY-Tamagotchi/Readme.md)** | Every viewer hatches their own Tamagotchi, or chat shares one. `!tamafeed`, `!tamaplay`, `!tamaclean`, `!tamasleep`, names and colours, and proper Tamagotchi moods (Hungry, Sleepy, Bored...). It grows up a bit every time you go live, and there's an animated OBS overlay for the chat's one | No |
| **[FFY Bounty](FFY-Bounty/Readme.md)** | Put a price on someone's head with `!bounty @someone 50`, claim it with `!bountyhunt @someone`, or hide with `!hideout`. Wanted posters and a hunter leaderboard too | No |
| **[FFY Mod Appreciation](FFY-ModAppreciation/Readme.md)** | Show your mods some love. Watch streaks, a Mod of the Stream, `!modchamps`, `!thankmod` and an OBS overlay. It never touches anyone's badges | No |
| **[FFY Counters](FFY-Counters/Readme.md)** | 20 counters, like `!pickle` (+1), `!pickles` (show it) and `!setpickle 5` | No |

## How to install one

If it's a seasonal or game pack, install **[FFY Commands](../FFYCommands/Readme.md)** first. Those packs live inside the **FFY-Seasonal** and **FFY-GamePacks** folders.

Download the add-on's file from its folder. In Streamer.bot, click **Import** at the top, drag the file into the box and hit **Import**.

Click an add-on's name in the table for its own guide. That covers everything else.
