# FFY Add-ons

Extra packs for your stream. Each add-on is its own import file, so install only the ones you want.

| Add-on | What it adds | Needs FFY Commands? |
|---|---|---|
| **[FFY Christmas](FFY-Christmas/Readme.md)** | Santa's naughty or nice list (results can last a day or longer), `!present`, `!grinch`, `!reindeer`, `!cracker`, `!snowball @someone`, Grinch of the Day and more. 16 commands | Yes |
| **[FFY Halloween](FFY-Halloween/Readme.md)** | `!costume`, `!pumpkin`, `!spooky`, `!candy`, `!trickortreat`, `!spook @someone`, plus Nightmare Before Christmas ones like `!jack`, `!oogieboogie` and `!whatsthis`. 17 commands | Yes |
| **[FFY ThanksGiving](FFY-ThanksGiving/Readme.md)** | `!turkey`, `!gobble`, `!pie`, `!thankful`, `!leftovers`, a `!wishbone @someone` duel, Turkey of the Day and more. 12 commands | Yes |
| **[FFY Gears Of War](FFY-Gears-Of-War/Readme.md)** | `!activereload`, `!gnasher`, `!lancer`, `!gearsweapon`, `!execution`, `!curbstomp @someone`, `!revive @someone`, a `!chainsaw @someone` duel, COG and Locust of the Day and more. 20 commands | Yes |
| **[FFY Daily's](FFY-Dailys/Readme.md)** | 20 daily channel point redeems, like *"Bob has redeemed their daily head pat!"*. Each viewer can claim each one once a day. Twitch only | No |
| **[FFY Pokemon](FFY-Pokemon/Readme.md)** | Open Pokémon booster packs (10 cards plus an Energy) with PSA grades and values. God packs, ten 1-1 Pikachus, `!keep` / `!sell all` / `!sell bulk`, `!binder`, `!bestcards`, `!pokemonwallet`, `!pokemontop`, and a season reset (`!pokemonseason`) after however many days you choose | No |
| **[FFY Dino](FFY-Dino/Readme.md)** | `!dig` for fossils, complete dinosaur skeletons for your `!museum`, amber and 1-1 legendary fossils, `!bones`, `!sellbones`, `!bestfossils` and a season reset | No |
| **[FFY Mining](FFY-Mining/Readme.md)** | `!mine` for ores and gems, `!sellores` for coins, `!upgrade` your pickaxe for better finds, BIG FIND diamonds, 1-1 gems, `!bestgems` and a season reset | No |
| **[FFY Pets](FFY-Pets/Readme.md)** | Pets for chat: dogs, cats, chickens, goats, llamas and more. One chat pet everyone shares, or every viewer adopts their own with `!registerpet`. `!feedpet`, `!walkpet`, `!playpet`, `!trainpet` (learn tricks), `!pettrick`, `!cuddlepet`, bond levels, and simple on/off switches | No |
| **[FFY Counters](FFY-Counters/Readme.md)** | 20 counters, like `!pickle` (+1), `!pickles` (show) and `!setpickle 5` | No |

## Installing an add-on

1. **Holiday and Gears add-ons** (Christmas, Halloween, ThanksGiving, Gears Of War) need **[FFY Commands](../FFYCommands/Readme.md)** installed first.
2. Download the add-on's file from its folder.
3. In Streamer.bot, click **Import** in the bar at the top, drag and drop the file into the box, and click **Import**.

Each add-on's guide (click its name above) explains everything else.
